## What is TrueFoundry?

* TrueFoundry is an **enterprise AI Gateway** that gives organizations one control plane to securely access, govern, and observe **LLMs, MCP servers, and agents**.
* The platform brings together **identity & access, routing & discovery, policies & guardrails, credentials & trust, usage & cost controls, and observability & audit** across your AI stack.

## One Gateway for Your Entire AI Stack

![TrueFoundry AI Gateway Architecture](./assets/Arch%20Diagram_Homepage.png)

* [**LLM Gateway**](https://www.truefoundry.com/docs/ai-gateway/intro-to-llm-gateway) - Access 1000+ commercial, open-source, and fine-tuned models through one interface with routing, fallbacks, caching, guardrails, budgets, access controls, and observability.

* [**MCP Gateway**](https://www.truefoundry.com/docs/ai-gateway/mcp/mcp-overview) - Secure and govern access to MCP servers and tools with centralized authentication, authorization, credential management, policies, and observability.

* [**Agent Gateway**](https://www.truefoundry.com/docs/ai-gateway/agents/agent-registry) - Register, discover, and govern agents with identity, access controls, policies, guardrails, cost controls, and end-to-end visibility.

**[TrueFoundry Docs →](https://www.truefoundry.com/docs/ai-gateway/quick-start)**

Explore guides, APIs, integrations, and deployment options for running the TrueFoundry AI Gateway as SaaS, in your VPC, on-prem, or in air-gapped environments.
